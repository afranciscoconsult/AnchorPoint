About Us

Anchor Point Cleaning Services provides dependable, high-quality residential and commercial cleaning throughout Rhode Island and Southern Massachusetts. We specialize in move-out cleanings, deep cleans, and property turnovers for homeowners, renters, and real estate professionals.
